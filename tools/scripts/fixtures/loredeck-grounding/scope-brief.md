# Scope Brief

## Fandom and source range

The Founding Trilogy, Book 1 (an invented canon used only by the grounding eval). Coverage runs from Mara Venn's arrival at Ravenhold Keep to the fall of Ravenhold at the end of Book 1.

## Continuity and canon tier

Primary canon: the Book 1 text only. Books 2 and 3, adaptations, and fan material are out of bounds.

## Deck split

A single deck, `grounding-eval`.

## Story-coordinate model

Story anchors from `timeline.json`: the opening, Mara's first patrol, the start of the siege, the exposure of Sethe's pact-mark, the fall of Ravenhold, and the end of Book 1.

## Spoiler philosophy

Sethe's oath to the Ashen Pact stays gated until his pact-mark is exposed. Deaths and the fall of Ravenhold stay gated until the fall.

## Assumptions and risks

None: the canon is fixed by the fixture evidence file.
